Return Eligibility

You may request a return within 14 calendar days of the date you receive your order. To be eligible for a return, items must be:

  • In their original, unused, and unaltered condition
  • In all original packaging, with tags attached where applicable
  • Accompanied by your order number or proof of purchase
  • Free from damage caused by the customer

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return due to their nature:

  • Perishable goods (food, beverages, fresh produce)
  • Personalised or custom-made items
  • Digital downloads and online memberships once activated
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase
  • Intimate or hygienic goods once opened (where applicable)
  • Hazardous materials or items restricted from return by Japanese law

How to Request a Return

To initiate a return, contact our customer team within your 14-day window:

  1. Email returns@japanprovenance.guild with your order number and reason for return
  2. Our team will respond within 2 business days with a Return Merchandise Authorisation (RMA) number and return instructions
  3. Pack your item securely, mark the RMA clearly on the outer packaging
  4. Ship to the address provided — we recommend a tracked service

Items returned without an RMA number, or returned after the 14-day window, may be refused or subject to a restocking fee at our discretion.
